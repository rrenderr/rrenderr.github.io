Delete your data

Kitty Steps by RPaha · Last updated: 4 October 2026

Kitty Steps has no accounts, so there is no account to close. Your game data lives on your phone, and a small amount of gameplay statistics and advertising data is held by our partners. This page explains how to delete both.

1. Delete the data on your device

Your cats, items, coins and gems, player name, step history and every other part of your progress are stored only on your device. To delete them, do either of the following:

  • Uninstall Kitty Steps, or
  • open Android Settings → Apps → Kitty Steps → Storage and tap Clear storage.

This deletes all progress immediately and permanently. We cannot restore it, because we never receive a copy.

2. Request deletion of data held by our partners

Our analytics partner (GameAnalytics) and advertising partners (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads) receive gameplay statistics, device information and advertising data, as described in our Privacy Policy. To ask for this data to be deleted:

  1. Email rpaha.games@gmail.com with the subject Kitty Steps data deletion request.
  2. Tell us your device model and roughly when you installed the game. Because there are no accounts, this helps us find the data that belongs to you. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

We will reply within 30 days. We delete everything we can link to you and forward the request to our partners. Some partners keep limited records for a short period where the law requires it, for example to prevent fraud, as described in their own privacy policies.
