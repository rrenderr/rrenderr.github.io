Kitty Steps

Every step you walk brings a new cat closer. Open mystery boxes, fill your collection book and make friends with dozens of cozy cats.

How it works

👟

Walk

Your phone's step counter powers the game. Put a box in the walking slot and every real step brings it closer to opening.

🎁

Discover

Open boxes to meet common, rare and epic cats from the Classic and Forest collections.

📖

Collect

Every new cat takes its place in your collection book, with its own story to unlock.

🐟

Care

Feed your cats, play with toys and grow your friendship to earn rewards.

🐾

Walk together

Pick two companion cats. They grow closer to you with every step you take together.

🗺️

Explore

Send your cats on expeditions across the map and bring back treats, toys and treasure.
